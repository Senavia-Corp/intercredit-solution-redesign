Introduction

At InterCredit Solution, a credit repair company and financial advisor, we are dedicated to protecting your privacy and ensuring the confidentiality of any personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data, and describes your rights regarding your personal information. It also clarifies our practices regarding cookies, third-party links, and updates to this policy.

Data Collection

We collect the following types of data:

Personal Information: This includes your name, contact information (e.g., email, phone number), credit and financial details, and any other data you provide related to credit repair and financial advisory services.

Usage Data: Information about how you interact with our services, including website visits, use of our financial advisory tools, or engagement with our educational resources.

Data Use

The information we collect is used for the following purposes:

Credit Repair and Financial Advisory Services: To assess your financial situation, provide tailored credit repair and advisory solutions, and support your financial goals.

Improving Our Offerings: To enhance the quality of our services, including educational resources such as the programming game concept under development.

Communication: To contact you about your credit repair or financial advisory process, provide service updates, and notify you of any changes to our Privacy Policy.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties except under the following circumstances:

Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to valid legal requests.

Note: SMS opt-in or phone numbers for the purpose of SMS communications are not shared with any third parties.

User Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Access: Request access to the personal information we hold about you.

Correction: Request corrections to any inaccurate or incomplete information.

Deletion: Request the deletion of your personal data, subject to any legal obligations that may require us to retain certain information.

Opting Out: Opt out of certain data collection practices, such as cookies or marketing communications.

Data Protection

We take appropriate security measures to protect your personal data, including encryption, secure servers, and firewalls. While we work diligently to ensure the safety of your information, please note that no system is entirely secure.

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties. If you wish to be removed from receiving future communications, you can opt out by texting STOP, QUIT, END, REVOKE, OPT OUT, CANCEL, or UNSUBSCRIBE.

Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and other tracking technologies to improve your experience on our website. These technologies help us understand how you use our services and allow us to offer personalized financial advice and credit repair solutions. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services, which are not governed by this Privacy Policy. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of any third-party sites before sharing personal information with them.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. If significant changes are made, we will notify you through appropriate channels, such as email or by posting the updated policy on our website. Please check this policy regularly to stay informed of any updates.
